# Cutroom

**Register:** product  
**Platform:** web (Electron desktop shell)

## Purpose

A private offline desktop desk for filmmakers, prompt engineers, and MiniMax H3 users who need production-ready Full-Reference (Ref2VA) prompts without sending briefs or reference stills to the cloud.

## Users

- Primary: solo creators and small studios crafting MiniMax Hailuo / H3 video prompts on Windows or Linux workstations with local GPUs (especially 24 GB cards).
- Secondary: technical artists validating Ref2VA retention labeling (`fully_preserved` / `partially_preserved` / `attribute_transfer` / `weak_reference`) before pasting into production tools.

## Brand personality

Quiet, precise, nocturnal edit-bay energy. Feels like LM Studio crossed with a high-end color suite — dense enough for work, never flashy. Confidence comes from clarity of controls and trustworthy streaming output, not ornament.

## Anti-references

- Purple-glow “AI SaaS” dashboards
- Chatbot bubbles as the primary workspace
- Cloud upload flows, account walls, telemetry banners
- Playful illustration-heavy empty states

## Product principles

1. **Offline is the product** — local models, local files, local library. No keys required.
2. **Format fidelity over cleverness** — always emit the official six-section Ref2VA contract.
3. **References stay tangible** — thumbnails, roles, traits, and optional vision captions live beside the brief.
4. **VRAM honesty** — warn when large unquantized models fight a 24 GB card.
5. **Tool disappears into the task** — familiar product density; accent reserved for status and primary actions.
