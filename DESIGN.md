# Design System — Cutroom

## Visual theme

Dark-only grading-suite UI. Warm umber neutrals with a tungsten copper accent for readiness and primary actions. Restrained product register: accent ≤10% of surface.

Physical scene: late-night color bay, tungsten practicals, deep shadow — warmth from light spill, not from a cream canvas.
